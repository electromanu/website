Who We Are

About Trimurti Facility Services

Founded in 2010, Trimurti Facility Services has grown from a small local provider to one of India's most trusted facility management companies — serving over 500 clients across residential complexes, commercial buildings, hotels, hospitals, and industrial facilities.

Our experienced workforce is trained to handle every aspect of facility management including security, maintenance, cleaning, and infrastructure support. Every project is handled with care, efficiency, and strict quality standards.

Modern organizations require well-managed infrastructure to ensure productivity and safety. We help reduce operational costs, maintain hygienic workplaces, and create better environments for everyone.
